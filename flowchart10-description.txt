The daily process from my daily life is "doing a task"

First, I read the task instructions and input the information from the task.

Then I decide whether I understand the task.

If I understand the task, I solve it and output the solution.

If I do not understand the task, I research the task further using a book or my notes. 

After researching, I check again whether I understand the task.

This process continues until I understand the task and can solve it.
